Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Marriage Proposal Punta Cana ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when inquiring about or booking our services. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and any information shared when you contact us via WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, or email.
  • Event Details: Dates, preferences, budget, and specific requests regarding your proposal planning.
  • Usage Data: Standard analytics data collected automatically through Google Analytics (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site) via cookies.

2. We Do Not Collect Financial Data

All payments for our services are processed securely through third-party platforms (such as PayPal, Zelle, or Modulebank). We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and customize your proposal packages.
  • Coordinate logistics, transfers, and vendors (photographers, decorators) for your event.
  • Improve our website performance and optimize our Google Ads campaigns.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We only share necessary details with trusted local vendors (e.g., your hotel name for the transfer coordinator or your first name for the photographer) strictly to execute your booked event.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience and analyze traffic via Google Analytics. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some website features may not function properly as a result.

6. Your Rights (US & International Visitors)

Depending on your location (including US states like California), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out of data collection via cookies by adjusting your browser settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly at the phone number or chat links provided on this website.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via WhatsApp.
