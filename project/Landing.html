Everything you need to know before you book.

Most couples book 2–6 weeks ahead, but we've pulled off beautiful proposals with as little as 48 hours' notice. High season (Dec–Apr) fills faster — message us early to lock your sunset slot on Bounty Beach.

Caribbean weather is fast-moving — rain rarely lasts more than an hour. We track the radar all day and stay in close touch. If a setup truly can't happen, you can reschedule free of charge to any other date that week (or get a full refund if you're leaving the island).

Our home base is Bounty Beach in Playa Bavaro — a semi-private stretch with a perfect west-facing sunset. We pick up from any resort in Bavaro, Cap Cana, Uvero Alto and Punta Cana Village.

Our photographers use long lenses from ~30m away — far enough she won't notice them, close enough to catch the exact micro-expression when she realises what's happening. After you say "yes" we move in for couple portraits.

A 30% deposit locks your date. The remainder is due 48 hours before your proposal. We accept Zelle, Venmo, bank transfers, and USD cash on the day. All official payment details and invoices are confirmed directly via WhatsApp or sent to your email at sofiayupiha@gmail.com.
